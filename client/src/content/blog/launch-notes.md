The web is full of things worth returning to: a tutorial, a thoughtful post, a product idea, or a tiny detail that solves a problem months later. SocialFeed Hub is starting with a simple promise: make those saves easy to collect, easy to find, and easy to share on your terms.

Today, the workspace brings browser bookmarks and social saves together in one searchable library. You can keep the collection private, add context while you save, organize it into folders, and publish only the links or posts that belong on your public profile. A profile at `/u/your-username` turns selected saves into a lightweight home for your ideas, with social links and a clean, shareable presentation.

This is the beginning rather than the finished destination. The next chapters will add richer collections, better import and browser-extension workflows, public profile customization, and a thoughtful way to discover what other people are curating. Pricing, dashboards, and more writing will follow as those pieces become useful enough to explain clearly.

We are building in the open and keeping the foundation deliberately focused: your library first, publishing when you choose, and features that help the internet feel a little less noisy.
